A note on negotiating vs. going to market: In the Canadian telecom industry, carriers are generally structured to retain customers at existing rates rather than proactively offer better ones. In most cases, businesses achieve significantly better outcomes by going to market — getting competing quotes — than by negotiating directly with their current provider. A competing offer is almost always your most effective leverage in any re-rate conversation.
